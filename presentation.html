The system

How it works

  1. RepairerStarts the jobEnters the plate, taps the damage
  2. AIReads it, picks partsOnly from the catalogue. Returns data, not screens
  3. RulesDecide who actsSure, cheap, returnable → added. Else → suggest or ask
  4. Design systemDraws the screen15 fixed components, one rules file
  5. RepairerChecks and sendsOne tap to undo, swap or approve
  6. RepairerFixes the carA step-by-step guide built from the quote

The AI decides what. The design system decides how.

The system

How the design system works

01 · GuidelinesWritten for people and the AI15 components, each with when to use it, a Do and a Don't. 15 rules in plain words.
02 · The filesOne source of truth
  • tokens.css colours, space, type
  • components.js the 15 components
  • RULES.md the rules
  • catalog.json parts and prices
03 · Checking the dataThe AI only picksPrice, stock and returns come from the catalogue. A part that isn't there is thrown away.
04 · Who controls the UIThe design system draws itThe AI sends data, never a screen. The screen checks the rules again, like the $150 limit.

After the quote

The repair guide: back to the craft

01Built from the quoteThe steps come from the parts on the quote and the car's trim, never a fixed list.
02It adds what the job needsA used part adds a check step. A sensor or camera adds a calibration step.
03One step at a timeWhat to do, how long it takes, one warning if there is a risk. Then Done.
04Closed with a photoThe final inspection needs a photo before the job can close.
